On the fix: I went back over it, and it does not fix the problem. Two
things remain open:

- mdev->dev->driver is checked and then used. Nothing pins the media
  driver's module during subdev_open(), as the cdev only holds
  videodev, so the driver can be unbound and its module unloaded
  between the READ_ONCE() and try_module_get(), and drv->owner is then
  read from freed memory.

- vdev->v4l2_dev->mdev is only valid as long as the driver keeps the
  v4l2_device and the media_device alive. vimc does, through its
  v4l2_device release callback, but ipu6-isys embeds both in a
  devm-allocated structure, so unbinding it frees them while an open()
  in progress or an open file handle can still reach them.

Patch 2/2 rests on the same vdev->v4l2_dev assumption, so I am
withdrawing the whole series rather than keeping half of it. The
underlying issue is the lifetime of the media device and of the
sub-devices when a driver is unbound with file handles open, and that
needs a proper fix, not another check in the file operations.
