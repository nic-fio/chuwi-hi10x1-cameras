This series adds drivers for the GalaxyCore GC5035 and GC8034 sensors,
the front and back cameras of the CHUWI Hi10 X1 tablet with IPU6, and the
matching ipu-bridge entries.

Hi Laurent, after reading your message, this is what I did:

 - No mode tables: the geometry is no longer in the register table, the
   readout window and the output crop are computed from the crop rectangle
   every time streaming starts.
 - Crop through the CROP selection. Vertically it is an analog crop (the
   readout window gets smaller and the frame gets shorter: the GC5035 at
   640x480 runs at up to 106 fps). Horizontally it is the sensor output
   crop, because narrowing the readout window horizontally gives black
   images on the GC5035, and on the GC8034 the column start register gives
   corrupted images at most values.
 - 2x2 binning on the GC5035 (patches 6 and 7): the 1296x972 format gives
   binning of the default crop, with the same field of view. The vendor
   sequence changes the PLL, so I measured the link frequency in that
   mode: 176.3 +/- 0.9 MHz, against the 175.2 MHz given by the PLL
   registers (with the same method, at full resolution, 420.7 +/- 1.5
   against 422.4). Patch 6 adds this frequency to ipu-bridge. On the
   GC8034 I only found binning in 2-lane vendor sequences; with 4 lanes it
   breaks the CSI-2 stream, so it isn't there.
 - I put the crop in patches 4 and 5 and the binning in 6 and 7,
   separately, after the IMX908 discussion about postponing them until the
   common raw sensor model
   (<20261001133138.GM944070@killaraus.ideasonboard.com>). If you want to
   do the same here, patches 1-3 stand on their own, at a fixed resolution
   like v1.
 - Some choices you may want changed:
   - set_fmt changes the crop: for userspace that does not set the crop
     first, it centres it on the default crop (as ov01a10 does). Also,
     1296x972 selects binning and sets the default crop back, unless the
     crop is already 1296x972. libcamera sets the largest size and then
     the one it wants, and with binning tied to the current crop it never
     got it. If you prefer binning through COMPOSE, or waiting for the
     common model, I'll change it.
   - In binning the timings are in sensor lines (the sensor reads one row
     of the array per line and outputs one every two lines): exposure and
     VBLANK keep the register unit, with a one line step, and the frame
     period computed from HBLANK and VBLANK is the measured one. This is
     why PIXEL_RATE is 70.08 MHz, HBLANK 164 and VBLANK at least 1024.
   - enum_frame_size lists 1296x972 separately, although it is within the
     full mode range, so that libcamera sees the binned mode.
   - The GC8034 PIXEL_RATE (256 MHz) is the one that gives the measured
     frame periods with the sensor line length; it is more than the 4-lane
     link carries (215 Mpixel/s), but over a whole line, blanking
     included, the average is about 196 Mpixel/s.
   - The analogue gain is the index of the sensor gain steps, which are
     not linear, as in the vendor code.
 - I don't have datasheets for these sensors, so I measured everything on
   the tablet: the areas that give image data (2608x1964 and 3282x2500,
   reported as NATIVE_SIZE and CROP_BOUNDS: they are not the physical
   arrays), the default crop (the same as v1: same image), the timing
   model (which corrected the GC8034 PIXEL_RATE to 256 MHz) and the size
   limits.
 - Testing: v4l2-compliance from git, 54/54 with no warnings on both (on
   the GC5035 at full resolution and in binning), a set of crop tests in
   two test sessions (rectangle, timing, position to the pixel), binning
   (timing, exposure, comparison with the per-colour 2x2 average of the
   full mode), libcamera 60/60 frames in three configurations per camera,
   and W=1, sparse, smatch, checkpatch and coccinelle clean on every
   patch. The test scripts and the raw measurements are available if
   useful.
 - Changes since v1:
   - patches 1 and 2: geometry removed from the register table and
     computed from the crop, all written as 16-bit values; NATIVE_SIZE and
     CROP_BOUNDS are the measured area instead of the vendor readout
     window; the default exposure no longer exceeds the maximum when
     VBLANK is reduced; GC8034: PIXEL_RATE set to 256 MHz and the blanking
     register computed from VBLANK; dropped linux/mod_devicetable.h and
     used a designated initializer in the ACPI table, as Uwe asked.
   - patch 3: added Dan's Reviewed-by.
   - patches 4, 5, 6 and 7: new.
 - I used an LLM (Claude) for the code and to plan and analyse the tests,
   as stated in the tags; the measurements were all taken on the tablet.
   The English of this letter is translated from my Italian.
